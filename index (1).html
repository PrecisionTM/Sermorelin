Who It's For

GH Decline Affects
More Than You Think.

Growth hormone naturally declines 15% per decade after age 30. By 50, most adults have less than half the GH levels they had at 25 — affecting energy, sleep, body composition, and cognitive clarity.

Chronic Fatigue

Low GH is directly linked to persistent low energy, poor motivation, and inability to recover between workouts or work days.

Poor Sleep Quality

GH is primarily released during deep sleep. As levels drop, sleep quality deteriorates — creating a vicious cycle of fatigue.

Loss of Lean Muscle

Without adequate GH, your body struggles to maintain and build lean muscle mass, even with consistent exercise.

Stubborn Body Fat

GH drives fat metabolism. Low levels cause preferential fat storage — especially around the abdomen — that won't budge with diet alone.

Honest Transparency

Who Sermorelin Is Not For

We believe in clinical honesty. Sermorelin is not the right fit for everyone — and your safety always comes first.

Under Age 30

Your pituitary is still producing peak GH naturally. Sermorelin is designed for adults experiencing age-related GH decline, typically beginning after 30.

Active Cancer or Malignancy

GH stimulation is contraindicated in patients with active cancer. Your physician will screen for this during your consultation before prescribing.

Pregnancy or Breastfeeding

Sermorelin is not indicated for use during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Safety in these populations has not been established.

Looking for Overnight Results

Sermorelin works with your body's natural rhythm — not against it. Results build over weeks and months. If you want a quick fix, this isn't it.

Diagnosed Pituitary Disorder

Patients with known pituitary tumors or dysfunction may not respond to Sermorelin as it requires a functional pituitary gland. Your doctor will evaluate this.

Unwilling to Self-Inject

Sermorelin is a subcutaneous injection taken at bedtime. If self-injection is a hard no for you, we'd recommend exploring our other oral-friendly programs.

Sermorelin is a synthetic analogue of growth hormone releasing hormone (GHRH) — the signal your hypothalamus naturally sends to your pituitary gland to produce growth hormone. As a 29-amino acid peptide, Sermorelin binds directly to GHRH receptors in the pituitary, triggering your body to produce and release its own natural GH. This is fundamentally different from synthetic HGH injections, which bypass your body's regulatory system. Sermorelin restores the natural GH-axis — so your body produces more GH in its normal pulsatile pattern, preserving the feedback mechanisms that prevent overproduction.
